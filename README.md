# 📚 GS Master App — Smart Bilingual Learning Portal

An ultra-premium, interactive, and comprehensive General Studies study platform for SSC and competitive examinations.

## 🌟 Key Features
- **🌐 100% Bilingual**: Seamless English + हिंदी content with interactive language mode switchers (EN, HI, or both).
- **🏛️ Complete Subject Modules**: Indian Polity, History, Geography, Economics, Science & Static GK.
- **📱 PWA & Mobile Ready**: Built-in lightweight local Wi-Fi server (`server.js` + `start-mobile.bat`) and PWA web manifest.
- **⚡ Interactive Tools**: Real-time search across all lectures, font size zoom, dark/light theme toggle, and collapsible Table of Contents drawer.
- **📝 Practice & PYQs**: Solved previous year questions with detailed explanations and interactive option checkers.

## 🏛️ Indian Polity Lectures Available
1. **Lecture 1**: Making of the Constitution (संविधान निर्माण) + Interim Govt & Committees
2. **Lecture 1 PYQ Set**: 26 Solved Previous Year Questions
3. **Lecture 2**: Salient Features of Indian Constitution & Schedules 1 to 12
4. **Lecture 3**: Preamble of the Constitution (उद्देशिका / प्रस्तावना)
5. **Lecture 4**: Part 1 & 2: Union & Its Territory and Citizenship (Arts 1–11 & 371A-J)
6. **Lecture 5**: Part 3: Fundamental Rights (Arts 12–35 & 5 Writs)
7. **Lecture 6**: Part 4 & 4A: DPSP & Fundamental Duties (Arts 36–51A & 11 Duties)
8. **Lecture 7**: Part 5: President & Vice-President of India (Arts 52–72)
9. **Lecture 8**: Part 5: Prime Minister, Council of Ministers & Attorney General (Arts 74–78)

## 🚀 How to Run
- **On PC / Laptop**: Open `index.html` directly in any web browser.
- **On Mobile Device**: Double-click `start-mobile.bat` on your PC and open the displayed local Wi-Fi URL on your mobile browser.
