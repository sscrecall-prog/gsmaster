President and Vice-President

भाग-5: संघ की कार्यपालिका — राष्ट्रपति एवं उपराष्ट्रपति (अनुच्छेद 52 से 72)
🏛️ Part V: Chapter 1 (Union Executive) 🗳️ Articles 52 to 62 (President of India) ⚖️ Articles 63 to 71 (Vice-President of India) 🛡️ Article 72 (5 Pardoning Powers) & Chronology

📜 1. Part V Chapters & Articles 52–53 (Union Executive)

Part V of the Constitution is Divided into 5 Chapters:

• Chapter 1: Union Executive (President, Vice-President, PM, Council of Ministers, Attorney General) [Arts 52–78]

• Chapter 2: Parliament (संसद) [Arts 79–122]

• Chapter 3: Legislative Powers of the President (Ordinance / अध्यादेश) [Art 123]

• Chapter 4: Union Judiciary (Supreme Court / उच्चतम न्यायालय) [Arts 124–147]

• Chapter 5: Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG / कैग) [Arts 148–151]

Article 52 : President of India (भारत का राष्ट्रपति)
  • There shall be a President of India.
  • He shall be the Head of the Union Executive.
  • Head of the Country / State (राष्ट्राध्यक्ष).
  • First Citizen of India (भारत का प्रथम नागरिक).
  • Nominal Head / Titular Head / De jure head of the Country (संवैधानिक प्रमुख).
Article 53 : Executive Power of the Union
  • Executive Power of the Union shall vest in the President and shall be exercised by him directly or through subordinate officers.
  • He is the Supreme Commander of all the Defence Forces (Army, Navy, Airforce) [तीनों सेनाओं का सर्वोच्च सेनापति].

🗳️ 2. Articles 54 & 55: Election of President & Vote Formulas

Article 54 : Electoral College (निर्वाचक मंडल)

Who Participates in the Election? (शामिल सदस्य):
  • 1. Elected members of both Houses of Parliament (Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha).
  • 2. Elected members of the Legislative Assemblies (MLAs) of States.
  • 3. Elected members of Legislative Assemblies of Delhi and Puducherry (70th CA 1992).
Who DO NOT Participate? (शामिल नहीं होते):
  • • Nominated members of Parliament (LS + RS).
  • • Members of State Legislative Councils (MLCs) [Both elected & nominated].
  • • Nominated members of State Assemblies & UT Assemblies.

Article 55 : Manner of Election (निर्वाचन की रीति)

  • Indirect Election (अप्रत्यक्ष निर्वाचन — जनता सीधे वोट नहीं देती).
  • Principle of Proportional Representation by means of Single Transferable Vote (STV) [आनुपातिक प्रतिनिधित्व प्रणाली एकल संक्रमणीय मत द्वारा]।
  • Voting done by Secret Ballot (गुप्त मतदान).
  • Requires 50 Proposers + 50 Seconders (50 प्रस्तावक व 50 अनुमोदक).
  • Security Deposit: ₹15,000 in RBI (forfeited if less than 1/6th valid votes polled).

Mathematical Formulas for Presidential Election:

Value of Vote of an MLA (विधायक के मत का मूल्य)
Value of MLA Vote = [Total State Population / Total Elected MLAs] × (1 / 1000)
Value of Vote of an MP (सांसद के मत का मूल्य)
Value of MP Vote = [Total Value of Votes of all MLAs of all States] / [Total Number of Elected MPs]
Electoral Quota Formula (निर्वाचित होने के लिए न्यूनतम कोटा)
Electoral Quota = [ (Total Number of Valid Votes Polled) / (1 + 1) ] + 1 = 50% + 1 Vote

📋 3. Articles 56 to 60: Term, Qualifications, Conditions & Oath

Article 56 : Term of Office (पदावधि)
  • Term is 5 Years from entering upon office.
  • Resigns by addressing letter to the Vice-President (who informs Speaker of Lok Sabha).
  • Can be removed by impeachment under Article 61.
Article 57 : Re-election Eligibility (पुनर्निर्वाचन)
  • Eligible for re-election any number of times (No term limit in India, unlike USA where limit is 2 terms).
  • Naturalised citizen can also become President in India (unlike USA where only natural-born citizen can become President).
Article 58 : Qualifications (अर्हताएं)
  • 1. Must be a Citizen of India.
  • 2. Minimum 35 years of age.
  • 3. Qualified for election as a member of Lok Sabha.
  • 4. Must not hold any Office of Profit under Govt.
Article 59 : Conditions of Office (शर्तें)
  • Shall not be a member of Parliament or State Legislature.
  • Entitled to Rashtrapati Bhavan without rent.
  • Emoluments cannot be diminished during his term.
Article 60 : Oath or Affirmation by the President (शपथ)

Administered by the Chief Justice of India (CJI). If CJI is unavailable, then by the Senior-Most Judge of the Supreme Court.

📌 Office of Profit (लाभ का पद):

Offices where government makes appointments/removals and pays salaries. Sitting President, VP, Governor, and Ministers are NOT considered as holding office of profit. (Term is not defined in Constitution).

🛡️ Legal Immunities (Article 361):

Immune from any criminal proceedings and arrest during office term. Civil proceedings can be instituted only after giving 2 months' written notice.

⚖️ 4. Article 61: Procedure for Impeachment of President

Ground of Impeachment:

"Violation of the Constitution" (संविधान का अतिक्रमण / उल्लंघन — This phrase is not defined in the Constitution).

Step-by-Step Impeachment Process:

Step 1: Initiation

Can be initiated in either House of Parliament (Lok Sabha or Rajya Sabha). Signed by 1/4th of the total members of that House.

Step 2: 14 Days Notice

A 14 days' advance written notice must be given to the President before the resolution is taken up.

Step 3: 1st House Passage

Resolution must be passed by a Special Majority of 2/3rd of the TOTAL Membership of that House.

Step 4: 2nd House Investigation & Removal

Sent to other House to investigate charges; President has the right to appear and be represented. If the 2nd House also passes the resolution by a Special Majority of 2/3rd of TOTAL Membership, the President stands removed from office on that date.

Key Facts on Impeachment:
  • • All Members of Parliament (Elected + Nominated) take part in impeachment.
  • • MLAs of States/UTs do NOT participate in impeachment (even though they vote in election).
  • • Impeachment is a Quasi-judicial process, borrowed from the USA.
  • • No President has been impeached in India till date.

💡 5. Article 62 (Vacancy in President's Office) & Articles 52–62 Trick

Article 62 : Time of Holding Election to Fill Vacancy
  • Normal Expiration: Election completed before expiration of term.
  • Casual Vacancy (Death/Resignation/Removal): Election must be held within 6 months.
Acting President Line of Succession

1. Vice-President acts as President.
2. If VP is unavailable ➔ Chief Justice of India (CJI).
3. If CJI is also unavailable ➔ Senior-most Judge of Supreme Court.

🏛️ 6. Vice-President of India [Articles 63 to 67]

Articles 63, 64 & 65 (Constitutional Role)
  • Art 63: There shall be a Vice-President of India.
  • Art 64: VP is the Ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha (राज्यसभा का पदेन सभापति). Holds 2nd highest constitutional post.
  • Art 65: VP acts as Acting President during vacancies for a maximum period of 6 months.
Article 66 : Election of Vice-President
  • Electoral College: All members of both Houses of Parliament (Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha, including nominated members). MLAs do NOT vote.
  • Method: Proportional Representation by STV, Secret Ballot.
  • Nomination: 20 Proposers + 20 Seconders (₹15,000 deposit).
  • Qualifications: Citizen, min 35 years age, qualified as a member of Rajya Sabha, no office of profit.
Article 67 : Term of Office & Removal of Vice-President
  • Term is 5 years. Resigns by addressing letter to the President.
  • Removal Process: Resolution can be initiated ONLY in Rajya Sabha. Requires 14 days' prior notice.
  • Must be passed by an Effective Majority in Rajya Sabha (majority of all then members) and Agreed to by Simple Majority in Lok Sabha.

📌 7. Articles 68 to 71 & Supreme Court Election Disputes

Article 68

Time of holding election to fill vacancy in VP's office (as soon as possible in casual vacancy).

Article 69

Oath or Affirmation by VP: Administered by the President or person appointed by him.

Article 70

Discharge of President's functions in other contingencies as provided by Parliament.

Article 71 : Matters Relating to Election of President and Vice-President

  • 1. All doubts and disputes arising out of presidential or vice-presidential elections are inquired into and decided exclusively by the Supreme Court of India, whose decision is final.
  • 2. If election is declared void by Supreme Court, acts done by President/VP before the date of declaration remain valid.
  • 3. Election cannot be challenged on ground of any vacancy in the electoral college.

🛡️ 8. Article 72: Pardoning Power of the President (5 Types)

The President has the power to grant pardons, reprieves, respites or remissions of punishment or to suspend, remit or commute sentences in all cases of: (1) Offences against Union Law, (2) Court Martial (Military Courts), (3) All sentences of Death.

1. Pardon (क्षमा)

Completely removes both the punishment and the conviction (absolves convict fully).

2. Commutation (लघुकरण)

Substitution of one form of punishment for a lighter form (e.g. Death sentence to Life imprisonment).

3. Remission (परिहार)

Reducing the period of sentence without changing its character (e.g. 2 years rigorous to 1 year).

4. Respite (विराम)

Awarding a lesser sentence due to special conditions (e.g. physical disability, pregnancy of woman offender).

5. Reprieve (प्रविलंबन)

Temporary stay of execution of a sentence (especially death sentence) to enable convict to seek pardon or commutation.

⚖️ President vs Governor (Art 161):

• President can pardon Death Sentences; Governor cannot.
• President can pardon punishments by Court Martial (Military courts); Governor cannot.

📜 Kehar Singh Case (1988):

Pardoning power of the President is not subject to judicial review on merits, unless the decision is arbitrary, mala fide, irrational, or discriminatory.

⭐ 9. Important Landmark Facts on Indian Presidents

Dr. Rajendra Prasad: Longest serving President of India (12 years, 1950–1962).
Dr. Zakir Hussain: 1st Muslim President; 1st President to die in office.
V. V. Giri: 1st Acting President to be elected President (elected in 2nd count).
Fakhruddin Ali Ahmed: Died in office (B.D. Jatti served as acting President).
Neelam Sanjiva Reddy: Only President elected unopposed.
Giani Zail Singh: 1st Sikh President (used pocket veto on Post Office Bill).
R. Venkataraman: Served with maximum number of Prime Ministers during coalition era.
K. R. Narayanan: First Dalit President of India.
Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam: Known as "Missile Man" & "People's President".
Pratibha Patil: First Woman President of India.
Droupadi Murmu: First Tribal President and 2nd Woman President of India.
