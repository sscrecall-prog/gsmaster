🗺️ 1. Part VI Overview & Super Article Mapping Formulas (+89 & +90)

💡 Super Memory Magic: Union to State Article Mapping Formulas:

Centre ke articles yaad hain to State ke articles automatically yaad ho jayenge!

  • • Art 74 (Union CoM) + 89 = Art 163 (State CoM)
  • • Art 75 (Ministers) + 89 = Art 164 (State Ministers)
  • • Art 76 (Attorney Gen) + 89 = Art 165 (Advocate Gen)
  • • Art 79 (Parliament) + 89 = Art 168 (State Legislature)
  • • Art 110 (Money Bill) + 89 = Art 199 (State Money Bill)

Formula 2: Add +90 (Articles 112 to 213)

🏛️ 2. Governor of States (Articles 153 to 160)

Article 153 : Governor of States

There shall be a Governor for each State. 7th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1956 allowed the appointment of the same person as Governor for 2 or more States.

Article 154 : Executive Power of State

Vests in the Governor; exercised directly or through subordinate officers (Head of State Executive).

Article 155 : Appointment of Governor

Appointed by the President by warrant under hand and seal (Directly appointed nominee of Centre; not elected).

Article 156 : Term of Office

Holds office during the pleasure of the President (No fixed security of tenure). Normal term: 5 years; resigns to President.

Article 157 : Qualifications

Must be a Citizen of India and have completed 35 years of age.

Article 159 : Oath or Affirmation

Administered by the Chief Justice of the High Court of the State (or senior-most HC judge in his absence).

📌 Conventions on Appointment:
  • 1. Should not belong to the state where appointed (outsider).
  • 2. Chief Minister of the state should be consulted.
🛡️ Article 158 Conditions:
  • No MP/MLA membership; no Office of Profit; Raj Bhavan without rent.
  • If serving multiple states, salary shared as per President's order.

⚖️ 3. Articles 161 to 167: Pardoning Power, Council of Ministers & Advocate General

Article 161 : Pardoning Power of Governor
  • Can grant pardons, reprieves, respites, remissions, or commute sentences for offences against State laws.
  • ❌ Cannot pardon Death Sentence (Only President can pardon death).
  • ❌ Cannot pardon Court Martial (Military) sentences.
Articles 163 & 164 : CoM & Discretion
  • Art 163: CoM headed by CM aids & advises Governor. Governor has Constitutional Discretionary Powers (advice not binding in discretionary matters).
  • Art 164: CM appointed by Governor; other ministers on CM's advice. Collective responsibility to Legislative Assembly.
Article 165 : Advocate General of State
  • Highest Law Officer of the State.
  • Appointed by Governor (must be qualified to be a High Court Judge).
  • Holds office during pleasure of the Governor.
Articles 166 & 167 : Business & CM Duties
  • Art 166: All executive actions expressed in Governor's name.
  • Art 167: CM's duty to communicate decisions and furnish information to the Governor.

🏢 4. Articles 168 & 169: 6 Bicameral States & Creation/Abolition of Council

Article 168: 6 States with Bicameral Legislature (विधान परिषद वाले 6 राज्य):

💡 Trick: `K U M B A T` (Karnataka, UP, Maharashtra, Bihar, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana)

1. Uttar Pradesh (100) 2. Maharashtra (78) 3. Bihar (75) 4. Karnataka (75) 5. Andhra Pradesh (58) 6. Telangana (40)
(8 केंद्र शासित प्रदेशों में से केवल 3 UTs में विधानसभा है: दिल्ली, पुडुचेरी एवं जम्मू-कश्मीर).

Article 169 : Abolition or Creation of Legislative Councils

Parliament can create or abolish a Legislative Council by Simple Majority, if the concerned State Legislative Assembly passes a resolution by a Special Majority (Majority of total membership + 2/3rd members present and voting).

• Such a law is NOT deemed to be an amendment under Article 368.

🟢 5. Article 170: Composition of Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha)

General Rules on Assembly Strength
  • Direct election by citizens based on Universal Adult Suffrage.
  • Maximum Strength: 500 Members.
  • Minimum Strength: 60 Members.
  • Normal Duration: 5 Years (Article 172).
  • Minimum Age to Contest: 25 Years (Article 173).
Special Exceptions (< 60 Seats)
  • • Sikkim: 32 Seats (Smallest Assembly in States)
  • • Goa: 40 Seats
  • • Mizoram: 40 Seats
  • • Nagaland: 60 (Statutory minimum 46)
  • • Arunachal Pradesh: 60 (Statutory minimum 30)
  • • Puducherry UT: 30 Elected Seats

🔴 6. Article 171: Composition of Legislative Council (5-Tier Electoral Formula)

Strength Limits: Maximum = 1/3rd of total strength of Legislative Assembly; Minimum = 40 Members. (Permanent body, 1/3rd retire every 2 years, individual term = 6 years, min age = 30 years).

5-Tier Composition of Legislative Council (1/3, 1/3, 1/12, 1/12, 1/6):

1. 1/3rd Members

Elected by Local Authorities (Municipalities, District Boards, Zila Parishads).

2. 1/3rd Members

Elected by Members of Legislative Assembly (MLAs) from non-MLAs.

3. 1/12th Members

Elected by Graduates of 3 years' standing residing in the state.

4. 1/12th Members

Elected by Teachers (minimum 3 years teaching in secondary schools/colleges).

5. 1/6th Members (Governor Nomination)

Nominated by the Governor from fields of: Literature, Science, Art, Cooperative Movement, Social Service (💡 `LiSAS + Co-operative`).

👥 7. Articles 178–190: Presiding Officers & Quorum (10 Members or 10%)

Assembly Officers (Arts 178–181)
  • Speaker & Deputy Speaker elected by Assembly.
  • Resign to each other; removed by Effective Majority (14 days notice).
  • Speaker continues in office till newly elected Assembly meets.
Council Officers (Arts 182–185)
  • Chairman & Deputy Chairman elected by Council from amongst its members.
  • (Unlike Rajya Sabha where VP is ex-officio, Vidhan Parishad Chairman IS a member of the Council).
Article 189: Quorum (गणपूर्ति):

Quorum for a meeting is 10 Members or 10% (1/10th) of total membership of the House, whichever is greater.

Article 190: 60 Days Absent Rule:

If a member is absent for 60 consecutive days without permission, the House may declare his seat vacant.

📜 8. Articles 196–198: Legislative Procedures & 4-Month Delay Rule (No Joint Sitting)

🚫 No Provision for Joint Sitting in State Legislature:

Unlike Parliament (Art 108), there is NO provision for a Joint Sitting in the State Legislature. The Legislative Assembly has the ultimate overriding authority.

Article 197: Maximum Delay by Legislative Council on Ordinary Bills (Total 4 Months):

1st Instance (पहला चरण)

When bill is sent to Council for the 1st time, Council can delay/keep pending for a maximum of 3 Months.

2nd Instance (दूसरा चरण)

If Assembly passes it a 2nd time and resends, Council can delay for a maximum of 1 Month.

Total Maximum Delay = 4 Months (3 + 1)

After 4 months, the bill is deemed to have been passed by both Houses in the form passed by the Assembly. Legislative Council is only a dilatory (delaying) chamber.

💰 Article 198 (Money Bills): Introduced only in Legislative Assembly. Legislative Council has only 14 days to return it with recommendations.

⚡ 9. Articles 200–213: Governor's Veto, State Budget & Ordinance Power (Art 213)

Article 200: Governor Assent

Governor can: (1) Assent, (2) Withhold, (3) Return for reconsideration, or (4) Reserve for President (Art 201).

Article 202: State Budget

Annual Financial Statement of State. Demands for grants (Art 203) voted only in Legislative Assembly.

Article 213: Governor Ordinance

Promulgated during recess of Legislature. Ceases 6 weeks from reassembly (Max life: 6 months + 6 weeks).

📌 Mandatory Reservation under Article 200:

Governor MUST reserve for the President any Bill passed by the State Legislature that endangers the constitutional position and powers of the High Court.

⚖️ 10. State Judiciary Preview: High Courts (Arts 214–231) & Subordinate Courts

Articles 214–231 : High Courts in States

High Courts for States, appointment of judges by President, writ jurisdiction under Article 226.

Articles 233–237 : Subordinate Courts

District Courts, Sessions Courts, appointment of district judges by Governor in consultation with High Court.
