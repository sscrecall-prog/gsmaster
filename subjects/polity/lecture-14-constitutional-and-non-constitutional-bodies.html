Constitutional & Non-Constitutional Bodies

संवैधानिक एवं गैर-संवैधानिक निकाय (ECI, UPSC, CAG, वित्त आयोग, NHRC, CVC, CBI, लोकपाल व ट्रिब्यूनल्स)
🏛️ Constitutional Bodies (ECI, UPSC, CAG, FC) ⚖️ Statutory Bodies (NHRC, CIC, CVC, Lokpal) 💡 Executive Bodies (NITI Aayog, CBI, Law Comm) 🏢 Administrative & Environmental Tribunals

📊 1. 3-Fold Classification of Institutional Bodies in India

1. Constitutional Bodies (संवैधानिक निकाय)

Explicitly mentioned in the Constitution under dedicated Articles. Any change requires a Constitutional Amendment (Art 368).
E.g., ECI (324), UPSC (315), CAG (148), FC (280), NCSC (338), NCST (338A), NCBC (338B), AG (76).

2. Statutory Bodies (सांविधिक निकाय)

Created by an Act of Parliament or State Legislature (not in original Constitution).
E.g., NHRC (Act 1993), CIC (RTI Act 2005), CVC (Act 2003), Lokpal (Act 2013), NCW (Act 1990), NGT (Act 2010), JSPSC.

3. Executive / Non-Statutory (कार्यकारी निकाय)

Created by an Executive Resolution / Cabinet Decision (Neither constitutional nor statutory).
E.g., NITI Aayog (2015), Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI - 1963), Law Commission of India.

🗳️ 2. Election Commission of India (Part XV: Articles 324 to 329)

Structure, Tenure & Removal
  • Permanent & independent constitutional body (est. 25 Jan 1950 — National Voters' Day).
  • Conducts elections to: Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha, State Assemblies (LA), State Councils (LC), President & Vice-President.
  • ❌ Does NOT conduct Panchayat & Municipality elections (SEC conducts those).
  • Composition: 1 Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) + 2 Election Commissioners. Multi-member since 1989.
  • Tenure: 6 Years or until 65 Years of age (whichever is earlier).
  • Removal: CEC removed in the same manner as a Supreme Court Judge; other ECs removed by President on recommendation of CEC.
  • 1st Chief Election Commissioner: Sukumar Sen.
Important Articles of Part XV
  • Art 324: Superintendence, direction, and control of elections vested in ECI.
  • Art 325: Single general electoral roll; no discrimination based on religion, race, caste, or sex.
  • Art 326: Elections based on Universal Adult Suffrage.
    ⭐ 61st Amendment Act, 1988: Reduced voting age from 21 years to 18 years.
  • Art 327: Power of Parliament to make laws on elections.
  • Art 328: Power of State Legislature to make laws on elections.
  • Art 329: Bar to interference by courts in electoral matters.

🏛️ 3. Public Service Commissions: UPSC & SPSC (Part XIV: Articles 315 to 323)

Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)
  • Known as the "Watchdog of Merit System in India".
  • Recruits for All India Services (IAS, IPS, IFoS) & Central Services Group A/B.
  • Appointed by President. At least 1/2 members must have 10+ yrs govt experience.
  • Tenure: 6 Years or until 65 Years of age.
  • Removal: By President on grounds of bankruptcy, infirmity, or misbehaviour (after SC inquiry).
  • Current Chairman: Dr. Ajay Kumar.
State Public Service Commission (SPSC) & JSPSC
  • Recruits for State Civil Services.
  • Appointed by the Governor of State.
  • Tenure: 6 Years or until 62 Years of age.
  • ⭐ Crucial Point: SPSC members are REMOVED by the PRESIDENT ONLY (Governor cannot remove them!).
  • Joint State PSC (JSPSC): Created by Act of Parliament upon request of states ➔ STATUTORY Body.
Art 315: PSC for Union & States
Art 316: Appointment & Term
Art 317: Removal & Suspension
Art 318: Regulations & Conditions
Art 319: Post-retirement Ban
Art 320: Functions of PSCs
Art 322: Expenses (Consolidated Fund)
Art 323: Annual Reports to Pres/Gov

💰 4. Finance Commission (Art 280) & GST Council (Art 279A)

Finance Commission (Article 280)
  • Quasi-judicial body constituted by President every 5 years.
  • Composition: Chairman + 4 Members (Eligible for reappointment).
  • Recommends distribution of net tax proceeds between Union & States and grants-in-aid (Art 275). Recommendations are advisory (not binding).
  • 1st FC (1951): K. C. Neogy
  • 14th FC: Y. V. Reddy | 15th FC: N. K. Singh
  • 16th FC (Current): Dr. Arvind Panagariya (Members: Ajay Narayan Jha, Annie George Mathew, Manoj Panda, Soumya Kanti Ghosh).
GST Council (Article 279A)
  • Inserted via 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016.
  • Joint constitutional forum of Centre & States for GST governance.
  • Chairperson: Union Finance Minister.
  • Members: Union MoS Finance + Finance/Taxation Ministers of all States.
  • Union Revenue Secretary acts as ex-officio Secretary.

👥 5. National Commissions: NCSC (338), NCST (338A) & NCBC (338B)

1. NCSC (Article 338)

National Commission for Scheduled Castes. Investigates rights violations of SCs and Anglo-Indians.

2. NCST (Article 338A)

National Commission for Scheduled Tribes. Created via 89th CA 2003 by bifurcating NCSC.

3. NCBC (Article 338B)

Conferred constitutional status via 102nd CA 2018. Originally statutory body est. 1993 (Mandal case).

Common Composition & Powers for NCSC, NCST & NCBC:

• Composition: 1 Chairperson + 1 Vice-Chairperson + 3 Members (Appointed by President by warrant under hand and seal).
• Powers: Vested with the powers of a Civil Court while investigating matters.

🗣️ Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities [Part XVII, Art 350B]: Inserted via 7th CA 1956. Appointed by President. *(Art 350A: Primary education in mother tongue)*.

📜 6. Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) & Law Officers

CAG of India (Part V: Articles 148–151)
  • Head of Indian Audit & Accounts Department. Single-member body.
  • "Guardian of the Public Purse" & "Most important officer under Constitution" (Dr. B.R. Ambedkar).
  • Acts as Guide, Friend & Philosopher to Public Accounts Committee (PAC).
  • Appointed by President; Tenure: 6 Years or 65 Years of age.
  • Removed in same manner as a Supreme Court Judge.
  • Art 149: Duties & powers | Art 151: Submits audit reports to President/Governor.
Law Officers: Attorney General & Advocate General
  • Attorney General for India [Art 76]:
    • Highest Law Officer of India.
    • Appointed by President (Qualified to be a Supreme Court Judge).
    • Assisted by Solicitor General & Additional Solicitors General (Statutory/Executive posts).
  • Advocate General for the State [Art 165]:
    • Highest Law Officer of the State.
    • Appointed by Governor (Qualified to be a High Court Judge).
    • Holds office during pleasure of Governor.

🛡️ 7. Statutory Commissions: NHRC, SHRC & Central Information Commission

1. NHRC (Est. 1993)

Under Human Rights Act 1993. Chairperson: Retd CJI or SC Judge + 5 members. Appointed by President on 6-member committee. Tenure: 3 Yrs or 70 Yrs. Current: Justice V. Ramasubramanian.

2. SHRC (Est. 1993)

Under Human Rights Act 1993. Chairperson (Retd CJ/Judge of HC) + members. Appointed by Governor. REMOVED by PRESIDENT. Tenure: 3 Yrs or 70 Yrs.

3. CIC (RTI Act 2005)

Central Information Commission. 1 Chief + max 10 ICs. Appointed by President (PM + LoP + Cabinet Minister). Tenure: 3/5 Yrs or 65 Yrs (Not eligible for reappointment). Current: Heeralal Samariya.

⚖️ 8. Anti-Corruption Bodies: CVC, CBI & Lokpal / Lokayuktas

Central Vigilance Commission (CVC)

Est: 1964 (Santhanam Committee). Given Statutory Status in 2003. Appointed by President (PM + HM + LoP). Tenure: 4 Yrs or 65 Yrs. Current: Praveen Kumar Srivastava.

Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI)

Est: 1963 (Executive Resolution, Santhanam Comm). Derives police powers from Delhi Special Police Establishment Act (DSPE), 1946. Executive body.

Lokpal & Lokayuktas (Act 2013)

Statutory ombudsman. Lokpal for Centre (Chairperson: Retd CJI/SC Judge + max 8 members; 50% Judicial). 1st: P.C. Ghose | Current: Ajay Manikrao Khanwilkar.

💡 9. NITI Aayog, Law Commission of India & NCW

NITI Aayog (Est. 1 January 2015)
  • National Institution for Transforming India (Replaced Planning Commission).
  • Status: Executive Body / Think Tank.
  • Chairperson: Prime Minister.
  • Governing Council: CMs of all States/UTs + Lt. Governors.
  • Vice-Chairperson: Suman K. Bery | CEO: B.V.R. Subrahmanyam.
  • Key Reports: SDG India Index, Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI), India Innovation Index.
Law Commission & National Commission for Women
  • Law Commission of India:
    • Status: Executive advisory body for legal reforms.
    • 1st Pre-Independence (1834): Lord Macaulay (Charter Act 1833).
    • 1st Post-Independence (1955): M. C. Setalvad.
  • National Commission for Women (NCW):
    • Status: Statutory Body (NCW Act, 1990 — Est. 31 Jan 1992).
    • Current Chairperson: Smt. Vijaya K. Rahatkar.

🏛️ 10. Key Quasi-Judicial Tribunals in India (Part XIV-A: Arts 323A–323B)

1. CAT (Est. 1985)

Central Administrative Tribunal [Art 323A]: Adjudicates recruitment and service disputes of central government employees.

2. NGT (Est. 2010)

National Green Tribunal (NGT Act 2010): Dedicated environmental court for speedy disposal of cases related to environmental protection & forests.

3. NCLT (Est. 2016)

National Company Law Tribunal (Companies Act 2013): Adjudicates company law disputes, mergers, and corporate insolvency (IBC).

4. AFT (Est. 2007)

Armed Forces Tribunal (AFT Act 2007): Adjudicates service disputes and appeals against Court Martial for Army, Navy & Air Force.

5. ITAT (Est. 1941)

Income Tax Appellate Tribunal: Oldest tribunal dealing with direct tax and income tax disputes.

6. RWDT (Est. 1956)

River Water Disputes Tribunal [Art 262]: Inter-State River Water Disputes Act 1956 for resolving water sharing disputes between states.
