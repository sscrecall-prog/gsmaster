💡 EXPLAINATION & DETAILED INTER-STATE COUNCIL ARCHITECTURE

Formation Basis: The establishment of the Inter-State Council was recommended by the Sarkaria Commission (1983).
First Council: The first Inter-State Council was officially formed in the year 1990.

🏛️ Inter-State Council Structure
👑 Chairman: The Prime Minister.
👥 Union Members:

Six Cabinet-rank ministers from the Union Council of Ministers (nominated by the PM).

🏛️ State Members:

Chief Ministers of all States and Union Territories with Assemblies, and Administrators of UTs without Assemblies.

Union Territories note: Union Territories that have legislative assemblies—specifically Delhi (D), Puducherry (P), and Jammu & Kashmir (J&K)—are represented by their Chief Ministers.
📋 Key Recommendations (Sarkaria Commission):
Article 356 (President’s Rule): Should be used only as a last resort, in extreme cases of constitutional breakdown.
Governor's Appointment/Role: Should be an eminent person from outside the state, not active in politics, and appointed in consultation with the State Chief Minister. They should have a fixed five-year tenure.
Inter-State Council: Established a permanent council under Article 263 (later formed in 1990) to act as a forum for dialogue, rather than a mere advisory body.
Fiscal Federalism: Increased financial resources and autonomy for states to reduce dependence on the Centre. It recommended sharing the net proceeds of corporation tax with states.
Administrative/Legislative Relations: Supported a "Strong Centre" for national unity but warned against over-centralization. The Centre should consult states when making laws on the Concurrent List.
All-India Services (IAS/IPS): Should be strengthened for better coordination between the Union and states
🗺️ The Five Councils & Regions:
