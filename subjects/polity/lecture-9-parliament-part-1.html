🏛️ 1. Article 79: Constitution of Parliament & Westminster Model

Article 79 : Constitution of Parliament (संसद का गठन)

There shall be a Parliament for the Union which shall consist of the President and two Houses to be known respectively as the Council of States (Rajya Sabha) and the House of the People (Lok Sabha).

संसद तीन अंगों से मिलकर बनती है: (1) राष्ट्रपति, (2) राज्यसभा (उच्च सदन), एवं (3) लोकसभा (निम्न सदन)। राष्ट्रपति संसद का अभिन्न अंग हैं क्योंकि उनकी स्वीकृति के बिना कोई विधेयक कानून नहीं बन सकता।
🟢 Lok Sabha (House of the People)
  • Non-Permanent House (Subject to dissolution).
  • 1st Chamber / Lower House / Popular House.
  • Constituted on: 17 April 1952 | 1st Sitting: 13 May 1952.
  • Members elected directly by the people.
  • Green carpet represents agriculture & grassroots representation.
🔴 Rajya Sabha (Council of States)
  • Permanent House (Never dissolved).
  • 2nd Chamber / Upper House / House of Elders.
  • Constituted on: 3 April 1952 | 1st Sitting: 13 May 1952.
  • Represents the States and Union Territories.
  • Red carpet represents royalty & sacrifices of freedom fighters.
📌 Origin & Names: Parliamentary system borrowed from UK (Westminster Model). Hindi names "Lok Sabha" and "Rajya Sabha" were officially adopted in 1954.

🔴 2. Article 80: Composition of Rajya Sabha (Council of States)

Strength of Rajya Sabha (सीट संरचना)
  • Maximum Strength: 250 Members
    • 238 Elected (States + UTs)
    • 12 Nominated by President from: Literature, Science, Arts, Social Service (💡 Trick: `LiSAS`).
  • Present Strength: 245 Members
    • 233 Elected (225 States + 8 UTs)
    • 12 Nominated
    (5 seats kept vacant for PoK, Aksai Chin, etc.)
Method of Election & Sources
  • Elected indirectly by elected MLAs of States by Proportional Representation with Single Transferable Vote (STV).
  • Election of RS Members: Borrowed from South Africa.
  • Nomination of 12 RS Members: Borrowed from Ireland.
  • Unequal representation based on State population (4th Schedule).

🟢 3. Article 81: Composition of Lok Sabha & 104th Amendment Act

Maximum & Present Strength
  • Maximum Strength: 550 Seats
    • 530 from States
    • 20 from Union Territories
  • Present Strength: 543 Elected Members
    • 524 from States
    • 19 from Union Territories
⭐ 104th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2019:

Abolished the nomination of 2 Anglo-Indian members to Lok Sabha by the President under Article 331.

104वें संशोधन द्वारा आंग्ल-भारतीय समुदाय के 2 सदस्यों के मनोनयन को समाप्त कर दिया गया; अतः अधिकतम संख्या 552 से घटकर 550 तथा वर्तमान संख्या 545 से 543 हो गई।

📊 4. Articles 82 & 83: Delimitation Commission & Duration of Houses

Article 82 : Delimitation Commission (परिसीमन)
  • Readjustment of constituencies after every Census.
  • 4 Delimitation Acts: 1952, 1962, 1972, 2002.
  • 42nd CA (1976): Froze total seats based on 1971 Census until year 2000.
  • 84th CA (2001): Extended seat freeze up to year 2026.
  • 87th CA (2003): Boundary adjustments based on 2001 Census.
Article 83 : Duration of Houses (अवधि)
  • Rajya Sabha: Permanent body; 1/3rd members retire every 2 years; term of an individual member is 6 years.
  • Lok Sabha: Normal term is 5 years. Maximum gap between sessions cannot exceed 6 months.
  • During National Emergency, LS term can be extended for 1 year at a time (max 6 months after emergency ends).

⚡ 5. Articles 84 & 85: Qualifications, Sessions, Motions & Daily Routine

Article 84: Minimum Age

• Rajya Sabha: 30 Years
• Lok Sabha: 25 Years
• Must be an Indian citizen.

3 Sessions of Parliament

1. Budget Session: Feb–May
2. Monsoon Session: July–Aug
3. Winter Session: Nov–Dec

Sittings Terminology

• Adjournment: By Presiding Officer
• Adjournment Sine Die: By Presiding Officer
• Prorogation & Dissolution: By President

Important Parliamentary Motions:

No-Confidence Motion (अविश्वास प्रस्ताव):

Moved only in Lok Sabha against entire Council of Ministers. Needs support of 50 members. No reason needed. If passed, Government must resign.

Censure Motion (निंदा प्रस्ताव):

Reason must be stated. Moved against individual minister or council for specific policy failure. If passed, resignation is NOT required.

Adjournment Motion (काम रोको प्रस्ताव):

Draws attention to an urgent matter of public importance. Needs 50 members' support. Censure element involved.

Calling Attention & Point of Order:

Calling attention to urgent matter (Indian innovation). Point of Order raised when rules are violated (no debate allowed).

Types of Questions:
  • • Starred (*): Oral answer, supplementary questions allowed.
  • • Unstarred: Written answer, no supplementaries.
  • • Short Notice: Urgent public matter (< 10 days notice).
Daily Routine of a Sitting:
  • • 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM: Question Hour (प्रश्न काल)
  • • 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM: Zero Hour (शून्य काल) — Indian innovation since 1962.

📜 6. Articles 86 to 92: President's Address & Rajya Sabha Officers

Article 87 : Special Address by President

President addresses joint sitting of Parliament at: (1) 1st session after each general election, and (2) 1st session of each fiscal year (Budget session). Followed by debate on Motion of Thanks.

Article 88 : Rights of Ministers & AG

Ministers & Attorney General have the right to speak and take part in proceedings of both Houses and committees, WITHOUT the right to vote.

Article 89 : RS Chairman & Deputy Chairman

• Chairman: Vice-President (Ex-officio, not a member of RS).
• Deputy Chairman: Elected by RS from amongst its members.

Articles 90–92 : Removal & Powers

Deputy Chairman removed by Effective Majority in RS. During removal, Chairman has NO right to vote (not a member); Deputy Chairman can vote in 1st instance (no casting vote).

⭐ Notable Fact: P. T. Usha became the first nominated woman MP appointed to the Rajya Sabha Panel of Vice-Chairpersons.

⚖️ 7. Articles 93 to 99: Speaker, Deputy Speaker & Pro-tem Speaker

Speaker & Deputy Speaker (Arts 93–96)
  • Elected by Lok Sabha members by Simple Majority.
  • 1st Speaker: Ganesh Vasudev Mavalankar (G.V. Mavalankar).
  • 1st Deputy Speaker: M. Ananthasayanam Ayyangar.
  • Resignation: Speaker resigns to Deputy Speaker and vice versa.
  • Removal: Effective Majority in Lok Sabha (14 days notice).
  • Speaker does not vacate office upon LS dissolution (continues till 1st meeting of new LS).
Pro-tem Speaker & Secretariat (Arts 98–99)
  • Pro-tem Speaker (Art 99): Senior-most member appointed by President to administer oath to new MPs and conduct Speaker election. *(e.g. Bhartruhari Mahtab in 2024)*.
  • Secretariat (Art 98): Each House has separate secretarial staff headed by Secretary-General.
  • Election Dates: Speaker's election date fixed by President; Deputy Speaker's by Speaker.

📌 8. Articles 100 & 101: Quorum, Casting Vote & Vacation of Seats

Article 100 : Casting Vote & Quorum

  • 100(1) Casting Vote: Presiding officer does not vote in 1st instance; votes ONLY in case of a Tie (बराबर मत होने पर निर्णायक मत).
  • 100(3) Quorum (गणपूर्ति): Minimum 1/10th of total strength of the House (55 in Lok Sabha, 25 in Rajya Sabha).
  • 100(4): If no quorum, House must be adjourned.

Article 101 : Vacation of Seats

  • Double membership in both Houses not permitted.
  • Simultaneous membership in Parliament & State Assembly not permitted (vacates in 14 days).
  • 60 Days Rule [101(4)]: If an MP is absent for 60 consecutive days without permission, seat may be declared vacant.

🚫 9. Article 102: Disqualifications for Membership & 10th Schedule

Article 102(1) : Constitutional Disqualifications
  • 1. Holds any Office of Profit under Govt.
  • 2. Declared of unsound mind by competent court.
  • 3. Undischarged insolvent (दिवालिया).
  • 4. Not a citizen of India or acquired foreign citizenship.
  • 5. Disqualified under RPA 1951 (e.g. jail term $\ge$ 2 yrs).
Article 102(2) : 10th Schedule (Anti-Defection)
  • • Voluntarily resigns from political party.
  • • Votes contrary to party whip without permission.
  • • Independent MP joins any party.
  • • Nominated MP joins a party after 6 months.
  • Merger Exception: When 2/3rd members agree to merge (91st CA 2003).
⚖️ Kihoto Hollohan vs Zachilhu Case (1992):

The decision of the Speaker/Chairman under the 10th Schedule is subject to Judicial Review by the High Courts and Supreme Court on grounds of mala fide, perversity, or violation of constitutional mandate.

🛡️ 10. Articles 103 to 106: Disqualification Authority, Privileges & Art 73 Recap

Article 103

Disqualification under Art 102(1) decided by President based on binding opinion of Election Commission.

Article 104

Penalty of ₹500 per day for sitting and voting when disqualified or before taking oath.

Article 105: Privileges

Freedom of speech in House; Civil arrest immunity 40 days before & after session (No criminal immunity).

📌 Recap from Executive: Article 73 (Extent of Union Executive Power):

Executive power of the Union is co-extensive with the legislative power of Parliament (Union List & Concurrent List) and includes rights, authority, and jurisdiction under international treaties.
